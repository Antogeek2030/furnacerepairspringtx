Furnaces rarely fail without warning. Paying attention to these signs can help you schedule repair before a complete breakdown.

## 1. No Heat or Insufficient Heat

The most obvious sign. If the blower runs but air stays cool, or the unit won't start at all, you need professional diagnosis.

## 2. Strange Noises

Banging, rattling, squealing, or grinding are not normal. They often indicate loose parts, failing motors, or delayed ignition.

## 3. Short Cycling

The furnace turns on and off repeatedly in short bursts. This wastes energy, increases wear, and usually points to airflow or thermostat problems.

## 4. Rising Energy Bills

A sudden increase in heating costs with no change in usage often means declining efficiency from dirty components or failing parts.

## 5. Uneven Heating

Some rooms are comfortable while others stay cold. Duct issues, blower problems, or zoning faults may be to blame.

## 6. Yellow or Flickering Pilot Flame

A healthy pilot should be steady and blue. Yellow or flickering flames can indicate combustion problems that need immediate attention.

## 7. Frequent Cycling of the Blower

If the fan runs constantly or stops and starts irregularly, the control board, limit switch, or thermostat may be failing.
